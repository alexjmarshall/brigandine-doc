Spells not assigned a type (as some spells appear on multiple lists). WRITTEN sources are assigned a type (either magic, illusionist, or cleric).

- Illusionists, Bards, and Jesters can read their spells from illusionist books/scrolls. Learning is automatic.
- Mages, Thieves, and Bards can read any magic book/scroll. Illusionists and Jesters can read their spells from magic books/scrolls. Learning requires a roll (Thieves can not learn).
- Clerics, Scribes, and Bards can read cleric scrolls.

logic when reading a book/scroll

- cast: does the class have the Read [Type] Scroll feature? if so they can cast.
  - mishap chance: can the class memorize this spell? if so, 0% mishap chance. If not, d20 + their spell skill vs. DC 10 + scroll spell level.
- learn: can the class read this type of book/scroll and is the spell on their list? if so they can learn

  - can read if they have the Read [Type] Scroll feature, otherwise see below
  - learning is automatic from cleric/druidic/illusionist books/scrolls, requires a roll to understand for magic books/scrolls

- cast from scrolls:
  - Cleric: clerics, cloistered clerics, and bards
  - Druidic: druids and bards
  - Illusionist: illusionists and jesters
  - Magic: mages, thieves, bards, and jesters
- learn from scrolls:

  - Cleric: couched in religious symbolism, requires a Religion roll
  - Druidic: secret non-magical language, requires Ancient Languages roll or Comprehend Languages spell
  - Illusionist: secret magical language, illusionists and jesters automatic, others require Read Magic
  - Magic: abstruse formulae, requires roll to learn spell for everyone

- cleric scrolls -- anyone can read

### Spellcasting Features

- Magic Spellcasting (includes ability to cast from Magic Scrolls, begin with 4 random spells known, choose 1 spell at level, others must be learned from books/scrolls)
  - if DELAYED (min level > 1), does NOT include ability to cast from scrolls and you do NOT automatically learn spells on level up, BUT you can cast while wearing armor you are proficient with without risk of failure
- Illusionist Spellcasting (includes ability to cast from Illusionist scrolls, begin with 3 random spells known, choose 1 spell at level, others must be learned from books/scrolls)
  - if DELAYED (min level > 1), does NOT include ability to cast from scrolls and you do NOT automatically learn spells on level up BUT you can cast while wearing armor you are proficient with without risk of failure
- Cleric Spellcasting (includes ability to cast from Cleric scrolls, and all spells divinely granted)
- Druid Spellcasting (includes ability to cast from Druidic scrolls, and all spells divinely granted)
- Incantatrix Spellcasting (includes ability to cast from Magic/Druidic/Illusionist scrolls if spell on list, begin with 3 random spells known, choose 1 spell at level, others must be learned from books/scrolls)
- Read Scrolls (cast spells from Magic/Druidic/Illusionist scrolls)
- Spellsongs (begin with 2 random songs, roll Learn Spell to choose one at level, otherwise 1 random one each level)
- Runecarving (begin with 2 random runes, roll Learn Spell to choose one at level, otherwise 1 random one each level)
- Scribe Scrolls (create scroll for any known spell)

## Spellcasting Level

- generally, a reference to level in the spell description that determines who or how many enemies it can affect refers to character level, while a reference to level that modifies duration/range/area/damage etc. refers to skill level
