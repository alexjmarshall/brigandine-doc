# Basics

## Core Rolls

**Skill Check:** d20 + skill bonus + standard ability modifier.

**Saving Throw:** d20 + save bonus + standard ability modifier.

**Ability Check:** d20 + full ability modifier.

A roll succeeds if it meets or exceeds the target number: the opponent’s Armor Class (AC) for weapon skill checks, or the task’s Difficulty Class (DC) for saving throws and other checks.

## Determining Difficulty

**Saving Throw vs. Special Attack:** 12 + ½ the monster’s Hit Dice (HD). E.g. the DC to resist the petrifying gaze of a Basilisk (HD 6) is 15.

**Saving Throw vs. Spell:** 14 + spell level. E.g. the DC to resist the effects of a confusion spell (level 4) is 18.

**Skill or Ability Check:** Determine the DC based on the task's difficulty, or refer to the Dungeon Level column for the typical DC at that level. If unsure, use a DC of 15.
