Parry/Counter/Dodge -- ATTACKER always WINS TIES

- Parry: roll parry die and add to your AC. If opponent misses, riposte for double damage, unless attacker impact + their attack roll is greater than your AC.
- Counter: roll your weapon speed vs. theirs. If you win, attack at +2. If you hit, their attack is disrupted. If they win, they attack at +2. If they hit, your attack is disrupted.
- Dodge: roll a Reflex save vs. their attack total to avoid. No attacking possible. If successful, their miss is a total miss.

Feint/Bind

- Feint:

  - vs. Parry: roll your weapon speed vs. their parry bonus. If you win, attack without defender's parry bonus or risk of riposte. If you lose, attack as normal vs. parry.
  - vs. Counter: halve your speed for speed contest.

- Bind:
  - vs. Parry: roll your parry die vs. theirs. If you win, attack with no parry bonus or chance of riposte. If they win, they may immediately riposte.
  - vs. Counter: use parry roll instead of speed for speed contest and remove counter attack bonuses.

## Facing

On your turn, you may freely change your facing before taking an action. Between your turns, you may change your facing once.

There's no mechanical difference between the front face and flank, only between front and rear. You can't target creatures or objects behind you with actions, spells, or attacks. Creatures behind you are **Unseen**, and therefore gain a +2 bonus to attack you.

To determine a creature’s front and rear areas, draw a line straight out to the left and right from the front edge or corner of its space. Any square where more than half lies on the far side of the line is considered part of the creature’s front area. All squares on the same side of the line as the creature are its rear area. The space the creature itself occupies is considered part of its front.

Some creatures have no facing because they can attack and perceive foes equally well in every direction. There's no advantage to attacking such a creature from a particular side.

## Concealment and Cover

### Concealment

**Partial Concealment:** You can be seen with difficulty. Environmental conditions like mist, heavy rain, soft foliage, or dim light provide partial concealment.

Creatures take a –2 penalty to **Spot** and **Search** partially concealed areas, and a –2 penalty to attack visible but partially concealed targets.

**Total Concealment:** You can't be seen at all. Environmental conditions like solid fog, dense foliage, or complete darkness provide total concealment. Creatures in total concealment are **Unseen** and **Blinded**. Line of sight is blocked but not line of effect.

### Cover

**Half Cover:** You're partly behind a solid object, like a narrow tree trunk, tipped table, or a corner you're attacking around. Gain +2 to AC, Reflex saves, and **Hide** checks.

**Three-Quarters Cover:** Most of your body is behind a solid object, like a thick tree trunk or a chest-high wall, or you're shooting through an arrow slit or peeking around a corner. Gain +5 to AC, Reflex saves, and **Hide** checks.

**Total Cover:** You're entirely behind a solid object. You are **Unseen**, and any attacks against you strike the cover instead. You can't attack from total cover, and it blocks both line of sight and line of effect.

**Low Cover:** A low obstacle (like a waist-high wall) becomes three-quarters cover if you kneel behind it, or total cover if you lie prone. An attacker ignores low cover if they're closer to the obstacle than their target is.

**Soft Cover:** If a creature stands between you and the target of your missile attack, and is the same size or larger than your target, it grants them +2 AC. This is known as soft cover. Soft cover doesn't provide a bonus to Reflex saves and doesn't facilitate hiding.

**Transparent Cover:** A solid but transparent barrier (like a _wall of force_) grants the bonuses to AC and Reflex saves, but doesn't facilitate hiding.

### Line of Effect and Line of Sight

**Line of Effect** is an unbroken, straight path to a target. It determines what an attack, spell, or special ability can affect. Line of effect is blocked by a solid barrier, but not by fog, darkness, or other immaterial visual obstructions.

**Line of Sight** is very similar to line of effect, but is blocked by anything that obstructs vision, like fog, darkness, or opaque objects.

Knockdown is save or be staggered, fail by more than 5 and prone. Trip is save or prone, fail by 5 or more and staggered. Push is like knockdown but you're moved 5' first. Pushing (like all grappling maneuvers only affects targets at most 1 size larger, and they get +4 to save).

At-will actions occur very quickly. At-will abilities, psionic disciplines, and spells with a casting time less than 1 _that don't require a targeting decision_ count as bonus actions. One bonus action can be done on your turn in addition to your standard action. Some of these can also be done during your opponent's turn as a reaction.
