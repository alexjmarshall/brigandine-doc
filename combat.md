Parry/Counter/Dodge -- ATTACKER always WINS TIES

- Parry: roll parry die and add to your AC. If opponent misses, riposte for double damage, unless attacker impact + their attack roll is greater than your AC.
- Counter: roll your weapon speed vs. theirs. If you win, attack at +2. If you hit, their attack is disrupted. If they win, they attack at +2. If they hit, your attack is disrupted.
- Dodge: roll a Reflex save vs. their attack result to avoid. No attacking possible. If successful, their miss is a total miss.

Feint/Bind

- Feint:

  - vs. Parry: roll your weapon speed vs. their parry bonus. If you win, attack without defender's parry bonus or risk of riposte. If you lose, attack as normal vs. parry.
  - vs. Counter: halve your speed for speed contest.

- Bind:
  - vs. Parry: roll your parry die vs. theirs. If you win, attack with no parry bonus or chance of riposte. If they win, they may immediately riposte.
  - vs. Counter: use parry roll instead of speed for speed contest and remove counter attack bonuses.
