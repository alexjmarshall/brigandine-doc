# Psionics

## Psionic Ability

Psionic talent is determined at character creation. Add together your full modifiers for Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma and roll this as a percentage chance. E.g. a character with Int 17 (+7), Wis 8 (–2), and Cha 15 (+5) has a `7 - 2 + 5 = 10%` chance of psionic talent.

Psionic ability is measured in **Psionic Strength Points (PSPs)**. PSPs are spent to use psionic disciplines and attack/defense modes in psionic combat.

**Starting Ability:** Find your _psionic modifer_ by adding together your standard modifiers for Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. E.g. Int 17 (+2), Wis 8 (-1), and Cha 15 (+2) results in a `2 - 1 + 2 = 3` psionic modifier. Then roll `2d10 + psionic mod` for starting PSPs.

**Advancement:** Each level after the first, increase PSPs by `1d10 + psionic mod`.

**Recovery of Psionic Strength Points:** Spent PSPs are fully restored after 8 hours of sleep. If less than a full night’s rest is obtained, recover 25% per 2 hours of sleep.

## Psionic Disciplines

Psionic disciplines are divided into five fields of study, each governed by a corresponding skill: _Clairsentience_, _Psychokinesis_, _Psychometabolism_, _Psychoportation_, and _Telepathy_. A character's _mastery level_ with a discipline is equal to their skill level in its governing field. E.g. The hypnosis discipline references your “level of mastery.” Because hypnosis falls under _Telepathy_, your mastery level is your current level in the _Telepathy_ skill. Minor disciplines are known as _devotions_ and major disciplines are known as _sciences_.

| Psionic Skill    | Minor Devotions                                                                         | Major Sciences                                                                                     |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clairsentience   | Clairaudience, Clairvoyance, Detection of Alignment, Detection of Magic, Object Reading | Aura Sight, Precognition, Sensitivity to Psychic Impressions                                       |
| Psychokinesis    | Expansion, Levitation, Molecular Agitation, Reduction                                   | Energy Control, Molecular Manipulation, Molecular Rearrangement, Telekinesis                       |
| Psychometabolism | Body Equilibrium, Body Weaponry, Cell Adjustment, Mind Over Body, Suspend Animation     | Aura Alteration, Body Control, Mental Surgery, Shape Alteration                                    |
| Telepathy        | Domination, Empathy, Hypnosis, Invisibility, Seduction, Telepathy                       | Mass Domination, Mind Bar, Telempathic Projection, Telepathic Projection                           |
| Psychoportation  | Dreamwalk, Time Shift, Anchor, Hologram                                                 | Astral Projection, Dimension Door, Dimension Walk, Etherealness, Probability Travel, Teleportation |

**Starting Disciplines:** At character creation, roll 1d6 and choose one devotion from the corresponding field:

| d6  | Psionic Skill    |
| :-: | ---------------- |
|  1  | Clairsentience   |
|  2  | Psychokinesis    |
|  3  | Psychometabolism |
|  4  | Psychoportation  |
|  5  | Telepathy        |
|  6  | (roll again)     |

**Advancement:** Each level, roll again and choose a devotion from the rolled skill.

- Starting at 6th level, you may choose a science instead, if you know at least one devotion from that skill.
- After 1st level, if you roll a 6, choose a discipline from the psionic skill in which your character has the highest skill level, instead of rolling again.

## Psionic Combat

Mental combat between psionic creatures occurs at twice the speed of physical combat. If you're attacking another psionic:

- You attack twice per round: once on your turn, and once between turns.
- It's advantageous to wait until just after your target has acted before attacking them on their turn, because their chosen action might limit their ability to defend themselves.

When a psionic attack occurs:

- The attacker and defender secretly choose their attack or defense mode, then reveal at the same time.
- If the defender is unaware of the attacker, they must rely on a previously activated _Thought Shield_, otherwise they're treated as defenseless (defense mode _None_). Non-psionic creatures are always treated as defenseless.
- See the Combat Table for the result by attack and defense mode.

If the defender is reduced to zero or fewer PSPs by the attack, they must immediately make a Will save or suffer the consequences in the defense mode _None_ column.

### Using Psionic Powers in Combat

Psionic disciplines are **at-will** abilities, and can generally be used on the same turn as another action. However, you can't activate a discipline and use a psionic attack mode on the same turn. You can attack psionically while _concentrating_ on a discipline, but can only concentrate on one at a time. Disciplines that require ongoing PSP expenditure generally require concentration.

Psionic defenses are activated freely in response to an incoming psionic attack, whether the target is you or an ally you want to protect within an area of effect. Only _Thought Shield_ is useful to activate outside of combat, as it serves as a defense against surprise attacks and prevents nearby psionic creatures from automatically recognizing you as psionic.

If you use _Psychic Crush_ or activate a psionic discipline, you may only defend with _Thought Shield_ for the rest of your turn. If you take a non-psionic action, you can't defend yourself psionically at all for the rest of your turn.

A creature incapable of voluntary action (due to unconsciousness, confusion, insanity, or whatever) can neither initiate nor be targeted in psionic combat.

### Attack Modes

| Attack Mode    | Point Cost | Range | Area of Effect   | Target Type  |
| -------------- | :--------: | :---: | ---------------- | ------------ |
| Psionic Blast  |     20     |  30'  | Cone             | Any          |
| Mind Thrust    |     4      |  80'  | One creature     | Psionic only |
| Ego Whip       |     7      |  60'  | 20' x 20' square | Psionic only |
| Id Insinuation |     10     | 120'  | One creature     | Psionic only |
| Psychic Crush  |     14     |  50'  | One creature     | Psionic only |

_Psionic Blast_ unleashes a wave of raw mental force, overwhelming the mind with a stunning revelation. It's the only attack mode usable on non-psionic creatures, though the attacker must have at least 100 PSPs remaining. Non-psionic targets are treated as defenseless. Psionic targets must save or be **Staggered** for 1 round, even if they have an active defense.

_Mind Thrust_ delivers a focused, piercing impulse that attempts to short-circuit the target’s synaptic pathways. It is an inexpensive attack with fair effectiveness against any defense.

_Ego Whip_ strikes at the target’s sense of self, inducing either crushing feelings of worthlessness or delusions of grandeur. It is somewhat less effective than _Mind Thrust_ overall (though very strong against _Mind Blank_), but can affect multiple opponents.

_Id Insinuation_ stirs the target’s subconscious into rebellion, setting primal urges in conflict with the conscious mind. It is more effective than _Mind Thrust_ or _Ego Whip_ overall, except against _Mind Blank_, where it is very weak.

_Psychic Crush_ overwhelms the target’s brain with a flood of mental signals, aiming to annihilate higher function through sheer neural overload. When using this attack, the attacker can defend only with _Thought Shield_ or not at all. Unlike the other attack modes, it does not reduce the target's PSPs, but has a flat chance of killing them outright.

### Defense Modes

| Defense Mode       | Point Cost | Area of Effect          | Save Bonus |
| ------------------ | :--------: | ----------------------- | :--------: |
| Mind Blank         |     1      | Personal                |     0      |
| Thought Shield     |     2      | Personal                |     0      |
| Mental Barrier     |     3      | Personal                |     0      |
| Intellect Fortress |     8      | 10' radius of defender  |     +2     |
| Tower of Iron Will |     10     | One creature within 60' |     +6     |

_Mind Blank_ veils the mind in obscurity, rendering its inner workings indistinct and difficult to target. It is primarily useful if you suspect an _Id Insinuation_ attack.

_Thought Shield_ shrouds the mind by masking one mental aspect at a time. Unlike other defenses, once activated it remains up indefinitely until brought down by a psionic attack. It is the only psionic defense possible during the same turn that you use the _Psychic Crush_ attack mode or activate a psionic discipline.

_Mental Barrier_ erects a wall of repetitive thoughts, exposing only a narrow sliver of the mind to intrusion. It is a good all-around defense, but protects only the user.

_Intellect Fortress_ summons the full strength of the ego and superego to repel mental assaults through sheer psychological resilience. It is useful for shielding allies as well as yourself.

_Tower of Iron Will_ draws solely upon the superego, constructing an impregnable bastion of discipline and resolve within the mind. It protects only one creature, but need not be the user.

**Defense Mode Overlap:** If a creature benefits from multiple defense modes (such as an active personal defense plus an area-of-effect defense) the best mode applies.

**Save Bonus:** Applies to all saving throws vs. psionic attacks.

### Starting Modes and Advancement

**Starting Modes:** All psionic characters begin knowing the defense mode of _Mind Blank_. Additionally, roll 1d6 for a starting attack mode:

| d6  | Psionic Attack Mode |
| :-: | ------------------- |
|  1  | Psionic Blast       |
|  2  | Mind Thrust         |
|  3  | Ego Whip            |
|  4  | Id Insinuation      |
|  5  | Psychic Crush       |
|  6  | (choose)            |

**Advancement:** At each level after the first, add one attack _or_ defense mode of your choice. All attack and defense modes are governed by the _Telepathy_ skill, and engaging in psionic combat improves it.

## Detection of Psionic Creatures

**Scan:** Psionic creatures automatically sense the presence of other conscious psionics within 60', unless the target has _Thought Shield_ active. In that case, the observer must _Scan_ for them with a DC 15 _Clairsentience_ skill check.

_Scan_ takes an action and functions in a 60' radius. It reveals which creatures are psionic, their current PSP totals, and which attack and defense modes they know.

Without _Scan_, psionically attacking a target only reveals the defense mode used (and it may be unclear whether the defense belongs to the target or an ally, as some defense modes have an area of effect).

**Monster Attraction:** Using psionic powers has a chance of attracting a psionic creature. If the next wandering monster check indicates an encounter, roll a d4: on a 1, it's psionic.
