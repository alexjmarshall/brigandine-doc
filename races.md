# Races

Subrace adjustments are cumulative with those of the base race, unless stated otherwise. _Example:_ the Duergar's class options include the base Dwarf's classes as well as their additional class (Cleric/Thief).

## Human

_Ardent for power and dominion. The heralds of a new age._

**Abilities:** +1 Strength, +1 Charisma

**Classes:** Any single or dual-class.

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common. _Bonus:_ any non-secret language.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Medium

**Ambitious:** Prime requisite XP bonus.

**Diplomatic:** +2 Leadership, Trading, and Reaction.

## Dwarf

_Dour and clannish but deeply loyal._

**Abilities:** +2 Constitution, -2 Charisma

**Classes:** Fighter, Berserker, Cleric, Runepriest, Thief, Fighter/Cleric, Fighter/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Dwarvish. _Bonus:_ Goblinish, Jotun, Kobold, Orcish, or Undercommon.

**Movement:** 9

**Size:** Medium

**Darkvision:** Can see up to 60' in darkness.

**Dwarven Combat Training:** +4 AC vs. Giant-type monsters (e.g. ogres, trolls, giants, titans). +1 to-hit orcs and goblinoids.

**Magic-Averse:** +4 to all saves vs. spells and spell-like effects. Magic items (except weapons, armor, shields, gauntlets, and girdles) have a 20% chance to fail when used. Roll each time the item is activated. Continuous-effect items that fail this check never function for the Dwarf.

**Small Arms:** Cannot use Huge-sized swords or bows.

**Stable:** +4 to save vs. knockdown, push, and trip attempts.

**Steady:** Unaffected by Light encumbrance.

**Stonecunning:** +2 Gemcraft and Stoneworking. Senses depth underground. Passively notices unusual stonework, such as stonework traps, unsafe ceilings, sloping passages, or sliding walls.

**Stubborn:** +20% XP required to next level.

**Trickwise:** +2 Lockpicking and Trap Disarming.

### Hill Dwarf

**Hardy:** +4 to save vs. poison and disease.

**Trader:** +2 Metalworking and Trading.

### Mountain Dwarf

**Runekeeper:** +2 History and Runelore.

**Tough:** +1 hitpoint per level.

### Gray Dwarf (Duergar)

**Classes:** Cleric/Thief

**Languages:** Automatically speaks Undercommon.

**Alert:** Passively notices stealthy enemies. Replaces _Dwarven Combat Training_.

**Distrusted:** -2 Reaction.

**Hard-Bitten:** Immune to paralyzation, mind-affecting illusions, and ingested poisons. +4 to save vs. insinuated poisons.

**Light Blindness:** -2 to attack rolls and Searching checks in bright light. Abrupt exposure to bright light blinds for 1 round.

**Shifty:** Can use each of these spell-like abilities once per day: _invisibility_ and _enlarge_.

## Elf

_An ancient race, aloof and preoccupied by their fate._

**Abilities:** +1 Intelligence, +1 Dexterity, -2 Constitution

**Classes:** Fighter, Swashbuckler, Cleric, Mage, Diviner, Enchanter, Thief, Assassin, Fighter/Cleric, Fighter/Mage, Fighter/Thief, Cleric/Mage, Mage/Thief, Fighter/Cleric/Mage, Fighter/Mage/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Elvish. _Bonus:_ Celestial, Centaur, Draconic, Gnollish, Goblinish, Kobold, Orcish, or Sylvan.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Medium

**Elven Weapon Familiarity:** +1 with Bow and Curved Sword. Can move and shoot a bow in the same round.

**Superior Vision:** Twice normal visual range in dim/bright light.

**Enigmatic Mind:** Immune to charm. +2 to saves vs. other enchantments.

**Keen Senses:** +2 Searching and Listening. Passively notices secret doors and concealed portals.

**Light Step:** +2 Hiding and Sneaking in wilderness terrain.

**Reverie:** Immune to magical sleep. Requires no sleep, but must spend 4 hours daily in a trance-like state to heal and recover spells. In this state, the character remains conscious and aware but taking any action disrupts thir rest.

**Weary:** +20% XP required to next level.

**Worldbound:** Cannot be raised or spoken with after death.

### High Elf

**Languages:** Automatically speaks Celestial.

**Loremaster:** +2 Medicine and Religion.

**Parting Gift:** Begins with a valuable item. d8: 1 – cloak of elvenkind, 2 – longbow strung of angel's hair (+1), 3 – hauberk of elven chain, 4 – waybread (12 weeks), 5 – rope of climbing, 6 – vial of starlight, 7 – silver flower bell, 8 – golden leaf belt.

### Wood Elf

**Abilities:** +1 Strength, -1 Intelligence

**Languages:** Automatically speaks Sylvan.

**Animal Empathy:** Befriends animals easily. Gently approach an animal to improve their Reaction by one category. Wild or hostile animals may save to resist.

**Woodland Stealth:** Hiding and Sneaking bonus increases to +4 in woodland.

**Worldsong:** Once per hour, may listen to their surroundings to sense traces of evil, extraplanar, or unnaturally hidden creatures, or echoes of powerful emotions (e.g. great sorrow or joy). In primeval forest, may ask a brief question of the Worldsong.

### Dark Elf (Drow)

**Abilities:** +1 Wisdom (females only), +1 Charisma (females only)

**Classes:** Cleric/Thief, Fighter/Cleric/Thief, Cleric/Mage/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Silent Speech and Undercommon. _Bonus:_ High Drow.

**Ambidextrous:** No off-hand attack penalty. When fighting with two weapons, both attack at the main-hand penalty. Replaces _Elven Weapon Familiarity_.

**Superior Darkvision:** Can see up to 120' in darkness. Replaces _Enhanced Vision_.

**Hated:** -4 Reaction.

**Light Blindness:** -2 to attack rolls and Searching checks in bright light. Abrupt exposure to bright light blinds for 1 round.

**Residual Magic Resistance:** +2 to all saves vs. spells and spell-like effects.

**Witchblood:** Can use each of these spell-like abilities once per day: _dancing lights_, _darkness_, and _faerie fire_.

## Half-Elf

_A restless wanderlust makes them natural explorers._

**Abilities:** +1 Dexterity, -1 Constitution, +1 Charisma

**Classes:** Fighter, Berserker, Cleric, Cloistered Cleric, Druid, Mage, Conjurer, Diviner, Enchanter, Transmuter, Ranger, Thief, Assassin, Swashbuckler, Fighter/Cleric, Fighter/Druid, Fighter/Mage, Fighter/Thief, Cleric/Mage, Cleric/Ranger, Druid/Mage, Mage/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Elvish. _Bonus:_ any non-secret language.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Medium

**Comely:** +2 Deception, Persuasion, and Reaction.

**Rootless:** +10% XP required to next level.

**Sharp Senses:** +2 Listening and Searching.

**Slippery Mind:** +2 to saves vs. enchantments.

### Half-High Elf

**Abilities:** +1 Intelligence

### Half-Wood Elf

**Abilities:** +1 Strength

### Half-Dark Elf

**Darkvision:** Can see up to 60' in darkness.

**Stigma:** Reaction modifier reduced to -2.

## Half-Ogre

_Foul-tempered and individualistic. Relishes mortal combat._

**Abilities:** +3 Strength, -2 Intelligence, -3 Charisma

**Classes:** Fighter, Berserker, Barbarian, Thief, Fighter/Thief, Barbarian/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Jotun. _Bonus:_ Abyssal, Gnollish, Goblinish, or Orcish.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Large

**Darkvision:** Can see up to 60' in darkness.

**Iron Stomach:** Can consume raw meat, rotten food or unclean water without risk of disease.

**Monstrous:** +4 Intimidation, -4 Reaction

## Half-Orc

_Some resist while others embrace their bestial nature._

**Abilities:** +2 Strength, -1 Intelligence, -1 Charisma

**Classes:** Fighter, Berserker, Swashbuckler, Barbarian, Cleric, Cloistered Cleric, Druid, Thief, Assassin, Paladin, Fighter/Cleric, Fighter/Druid, Fighter/Thief, Barbarian/Cleric, Barbarian/Thief, Cleric/Thief, Druid/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Orcish. _Bonus:_ any non-secret language.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Medium

**Beast-Marked:** +2 Intimidation, -2 Reaction.

**Darkvision:** Can see up to 60' in darkness.

**Killer Instinct:** +2 to hit injured or bleeding opponents.

## Halfling

_Garrulous and happy-go-lucky. Often underestimated and capable of great bravery._

**Abilities:** -2 Strength, +2 Dexterity

**Classes:** Fighter, Cleric, Druid, Thief, Guardian, Fighter/Cleric, Fighter/Druid, Fighter/Thief, Druid/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common. _Bonus:_ Dwarvish, Elvish, Goblinish, Orcish, or Sylvan.

**Movement:** 9

**Size:** Small

**Doughty:** +4 to saves vs. fear.

**Light Step:** +2 Hiding and Sneaking in wilderness terrain.

**Uncanny Shot:** +2 with Bow and Sling.

### Stout

**Darkvision:** Can see up to 60' in darkness.

**Direction Sense:** Senses direction of travel underground. Passively notices changes in direction or position caused by navigational traps, such as shifting rooms, sloping passages, or teleporters.

**Steady:** Unaffected by Light encumbrance.

### Tallfellow

**Movement:** 12

**Sharp Senses:** +2 Listening and Searching.

## Pixie

_Mischievous and naive. Fascinated by things novel or intricate._

**Abilities:** -3 Strength, +3 Dexterity, -2 Wisdom

**Classes:** Fighter, Druid, Illusionist, Thief, Fighter/Druid, Fighter/Illusionist, Fighter/Thief, Druid/Illusionist, Druid/Thief, Illusionist/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Sylvan. _Bonus:_ Centaur, Dwarvish, Elvish, Gnollish, Goblinish, Kobold, or Orcish.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Tiny

**Natural Flight:** Can fly at will.

**Natural Invisibility:** Can become invisible at will, and attack or cast spells while invisible.

**Pixie Dust:** Three times per day, blow magic dust upon a creature to cause the effects of one of the following spells: _confusion_, _fly_, _forget_, _invisibility_, or _sleep_.
