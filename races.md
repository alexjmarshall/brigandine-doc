## Dwarf

_Dour and clannish but deeply loyal._

**Abilities:** +2 Constitution, -2 Charisma

**Classes:** Fighter, Berserker, Cleric, Runepriest, Thief, Fighter/Cleric, Fighter/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Dwarvish. _Bonus:_ Goblinish, Jotun, Kobold, Orcish, or Undercommon.

**Movement:** 9

**Size:** Medium

**Darkvision:** Can see up to 60 ft. in darkness.

**Dwarven Combat Training:** +2 AC vs. Giant-type monsters (e.g., ogres, trolls, giants, titans). +2 to-hit orcs and goblinoids.

**Magic-Averse:** +4 to all saving throws vs. spells and spell-like effects. Magic items (except weapons, armor, shields, gauntlets, and girdles) have a 20% chance to fail when used. Roll each time the item is activated. For continuous-effect items, roll once when the item is first worn or used. On a failure, it never functions for the dwarf.

**Small Arms:** Cannot use Huge-sized swords or bows.

**Steady:** Unaffected by [Light] encumbrance. +4 to save vs. knockdown, push, or trip attempts.

**Stonecunning:** +2 Gemcraft and Stoneworking. Senses approximate depth underground. Passively notices unusual stonework, such as stonework traps, unsafe ceilings, sloping passages, or sliding walls.

**Stubborn:** +20% XP required to next level.

**Trickwise:** +2 Lockpicking and Trap Disarming.

### Hill Dwarf

**Hardy:** +4 to save vs. poison and disease.

**Trader:** +2 Metalworking and Trading.

### Mountain Dwarf

**Runekeeper:** +2 History and Runelore.

**Robust:** +1 hitpoint per level.

### Gray Dwarf (Duergar)

**Classes:** As base Dwarf, plus Cleric/Thief.

**Languages:** As base Dwarf, plus Undercommon (automatic).

**Alert:** Passively notices stealthy enemies. Replaces _Dwarven Combat Training_.

**Distrusted:** -2 Reaction.

**Hard-Bitten:** Immune to paralyzation, mind-affecting illusions, and ingested poisons. +4 to save vs. insinuated poisons.

**Light Blindness:** -2 to attack rolls and Searching checks in bright light. Abrupt exposure to bright light blinds for 1 round.

**Shifty:** Can use each of these spell-like abilities once a day: _invisibility_ and _enlarge_.

## Elf

_An ancient race, aloof and preoccupied by their fate._

**Abilities:** +1 Intelligence, +1 Dexterity, -2 Constitution

**Classes:** Fighter, Swashbuckler, Cleric, Mage, Diviner, Enchanter, Thief, Assassin, Fighter/Cleric, Fighter/Mage, Fighter/Thief, Cleric/Mage, Mage/Thief, Fighter/Cleric/Mage, Fighter/Mage/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Elvish. _Bonus:_ Celestial, Centaur, Draconic, Gnollish, Goblinish, Kobold, Orcish, or Sylvan.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Medium

**Elven Weapon Familiarity:** +1 with Bow and Curved Sword. Can move and shoot a bow in the same round.

**Superior Vision:** Twice normal visual range in dim/bright light.

**Enigmatic Mind:** Immune to magical charm. +2 to saving throws vs. other enchantments.

**Keen Senses:** +2 Searching and Listening. Passively notices secret doors and concealed portals.

**Light Step:** +2 Hiding and Sneaking in wilderness terrain.

**Reverie:** Immune to magical sleep. Requires no sleep, but must spend 4 hours daily in a trance-like state to heal and recover spells. In this state, the character remains conscious and aware but taking any action disrupts thir rest.

**World-weary:** +20% XP required to next level.

**Worldbound:** Cannot be raised or spoken with after death.

### High Elf

**Languages:** As base Elf, plus Celestial (automatic).

**Loremaster:** +2 Medicine and Religion.

**Parting Gift:** Begins with a valuable item.

### Wood Elf

**Abilities:** As base Elf, plus +1 Strength and -1 Intelligence.

**Languages:** As base Elf, plus Sylvan (automatic).

**Animal Empathy:** Befriends animals easily. Gently approach an animal to improve their Reaction by one category. Wild or hostile animals may save to resist.

**Woodland Stealth:** Hiding and Sneaking bonus increases to +4 in woodland.

**Worldsong:** Once per hour, may listen to their surroundings to sense traces of evil or extraplanar creatures, or residue of powerful emotions (e.g., great sorrow or joy). In primeval forest, may ask a single question, though the answer is nonverbal and requires interpretation. TODO revise

### Dark Elf (Drow)

**Classes:** As base Elf, plus Cleric/Thief, Fighter/Cleric/Thief, and Cleric/Mage/Thief.

**Languages:** As base Elf, plus Silent Speech and Undercommon (automatic), and High Drow (bonus).

**Ambidextrous:** No off-hand attack penalty. When fighting with two weapons, both attack at the main-hand penalty. Replaces _Elven Weapon Familiarity_.

**Hated:** -4 Reaction.

**Light Blindness:** -2 to attack rolls and Searching checks in bright light. Abrupt exposure to bright light blinds for 1 round.

**Residual Magic Resistance:** +2 to all saving throws vs. spells and spell-like effects.

**Superior Darkvision:** Can see up to 120 ft. in darkness. Replaces _Enhanced Vision_.

**Witchblood:** Can use each of these spell-like abilities once a day: _dancing lights_, _darkness_, and _faerie fire_.

## Half-Ogre

_Foul-tempered and individualistic. Relishes mortal combat._

**Abilities:** +3 Strength, -2 Intelligence, -3 Charisma

**Classes:** Fighter, Berserker, Barbarian, Thief, Fighter/Thief, Barbarian/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Jotun. _Bonus:_ Abyssal, Gnollish, Goblinish, or Orcish.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Large

**Darkvision:** Can see up to 60 ft. in darkness.

**Iron Stomach:** Can consume raw meat, rotten food or unclean water without risk of disease.

**Monstrous:** +4 Intimidation, -4 Reaction

**Robust:** +1 hitpoint per level.

## Pixie

_Mischievous and naive. Fascinated by things novel or intricate._

**Abilities:** -3 Strength, +3 Dexterity, -2 Wisdom

**Classes:** Fighter, Druid, Illusionist, Thief, Fighter/Druid, Fighter/Illusionist, Fighter/Thief, Druid/Illusionist, Druid/Thief, Illusionist/Thief

**Languages:** _Automatic:_ Common and Sylvan. _Bonus:_ Centaur, Dwarvish, Elvish, Gnollish, Goblinish, Kobold, or Orcish.

**Movement:** 12

**Size:** Tiny

**Delicate:** -1 hitpoint per level.

**Natural Flight:** Can fly at will.

**Natural Invisibility:** Can become invisible at will, and attack or cast spells while invisible.

**Pixie Dust:** Three times a day, blow magic dust upon a creature to cause the effects of one of the following spells: _confusion_, _fly_, _forget_, _invisibility_, or _sleep_.
