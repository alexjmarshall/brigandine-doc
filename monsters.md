## Special Senses

### Blindsense

A creature with Blindsense has a limited ability to detect the presence and location of creatures within its range, even if they're invisible or in magical darkness, as long as it has line of effect. However, targets detected with this ability remain **Unseen**. Blindsense is commonly found in animals that navigate by echolocation or detect heat, like bats and snakes.

Blindsense typically requires no action to use. If it must be activated, the creature gains its benefits only during its turn.

### Blindsight

Blindsight is the extraordinary ability to perceive your surroundings in detail without vision as we know it. It ignores invisibility and magical darkness within range of its ability, but it must have line of effect to see creatures. Ethereal creatures can't be seen. Blindsight typically implies innate magical or psychic ability.

Blindsight typically requires no action to use. If it must be activated, the creature gains its benefits only during its turn.

### Darkvision

Creatures with darkvision see in darkness as if it were dim light. Darkvision doesn't reveal invisible things, nor does it penetrate magical darkness.

### Keen Scent

A creature with the Scent skill has a much keener sense of smell than humans. They can detect the presence of other creatures with a DC 10 Scent check (for a passive check, roll an unmodified d20 vs. the sensing creature's passive Scent). On a success, the creature normally senses only the source's presence and general direction. However, within 10', success reveals its precise location.

Scent checks take a –2 penalty beyond 30 feet and –5 beyond 60 feet. Apply a +5 bonus if the source is upwind, or a –5 penalty if downwind.

After detecting a scent, the creature can make an active Scent check as part of its movement to follow the scent trail to its source. Movement while tracking by scent is limited to one-third normal.

Running water ruins scent trails for air-breathing creatures. However, water-breathing creatures with this ability use it in water easily.

Powerful odors easily mask weaker scents, spoiling the ability to detect or identify creatures.

### Tremorsense

While a creature with Tremorsense is in contact with a surface (such as the ground), it automatically senses the location of any other creatures within range that are moving on the same surface.

As long as the other creatures are taking physical actions, including casting spells with somatic components, they’re considered moving. They don’t have to move from place to place to be detected.

Aquatic creatures with Tremorsense sense the location of creatures moving through the water.

## Dragons

Always use highest of HD range, Age Categories simply young (2 HP/HD), adult (4 HP/HD), old (6 HP/HD), and ancient (8 HP/HD). Young are size L, adult and old are size H, ancient is size G. White and Black seldom live beyond old age in the wild. Modify base claw/bite damage by +1 for adult, +2 for old, +3 for ancient. Adult and up have fear aura, but save bonus of +5/+2 for adult and old.

Size H and G dragons can tail slap behind them (claw damage as blunt to 2/3 rear targets, respectively).

Size H dragons can attempt to grab and carry an enemy up to size L. Size G can grab two at once.
