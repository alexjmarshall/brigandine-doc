## Conditions

### Blinded

You can't see, automatically fail **Spot** checks, and all creatures are **Unseen** to you.

### Deafened

You can't hear, automatically fail **Listen** checks, and **Unseen** creatures don't need to **Sneak** around you to become **Undetected**.

### Invisible

You're magically transparent and therefore **Unseen** to every creature (unless they have a special sense or magical ability to see the invisible).

### Silenced

You emanate no sound. You can't cast spells with a Verbal component. If you're **Unseen**, you can move to a different location and become **Undetected** without **Sneaking** (unless your enemies have a special way to pinpoint your location without hearing you).

### Dazed

You can take a partial move action but not a standard action while regaining your bearings. This condition lasts for 1 round, unless stated otherwise.

### Stunned

You drop everything held, can't take any actions, and are attacked at +2.
