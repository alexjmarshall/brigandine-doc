## Fighter

**Hit Die:** d10

**Prime Requisite:** Strength

**Requirements:** Str 9

**Armor:** Heavy

**Shields:** Any

**Weapon Class:** Martial

**Specialized Skills:** Axe, Bludgeon, Curved Sword, Dagger, Flail, Hand-to-Hand, Polearm, Spear, Staff, Straight Sword

**Multi-Attack:** Attacks twice at 7th level, three times at 13th level.

**Lordship:** At 9th level, granted the title of Lord and a domain from a patron or liege. They may establish a stronghold by clearing the domain of monsters and building a castle. A stronghold attracts a company of soldiers to serve as garrison and peasant families to generate revenue based on the size and quality of the land.

### Fighter

_A stalwart champion of the battlefield._

**Chain Attack:** After slaying at least one enemy with an attack action, make one extra attack against a different enemy within reach. One extra attack is granted, even if the first attack action killed multiple enemies. _Example:_ a Fighter makes a sweep attack against 3 foes, killing the first and second, but not the third. The attacker is granted a chain attack against the third, which kills them off as well.

### Berserker

_A terrifying warrior who fights with reckless abandon._

**Frenzy:** Can fly into a battle rage a limited number of times per day. Gain an extra attack each round, +4 to saves vs. mind-affecting attacks, and temporary HP and negative FP equal to 5 + 2/level. Cannot cast spells or retreat from battle while berserk. Lasts a maximum number of rounds equal to Constitution score, or until the battle is over. When the frenzy ends, lose HP and gain FP equal to their temporary bonus. This may result in unconsciousness and shock, or long-term fatigue, depending on the resulting HP and FP totals. TODO double check FP buffer amounts

### Kensai

_An ascetic who seeks perfection in their devotion to a single weapon._

**Disciplined Defense:** May not wear armor or shields. However, begins with a +2 bonus to natural AC that improves with level.

**Weapon Focus:** Select a single melee weapon to master. Attacks with this weapon receive a bonus to attack and damage that improves with level. Requires at least 2 hours practising weapon forms per day, or 1 hour with a sparring partner. Missed practice sessions must be made up before advancing in level, at a maximum of 4 hours per day.

**Ki:** Can focus life energy to power their Kai or Whirlwind Attack abilities a limited number of times per day.

**Kai:** Spend 1 Ki point before an attack roll. If it hits, it automatically deals maximum weapon damage.

**Whirlwind Attack:** Spend Ki to attack multiple enemies in reach with a single action. Each attack beyond the first costs 1 Ki point.
