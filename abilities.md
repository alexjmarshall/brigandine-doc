# Abilities

## Strength (Str)

Brawn and physical might. Add the standard mod to:

- Attack rolls with axe, bludgeon, hand-to-hand, polearm and staff
- Damage rolls with all melee and thrown weapons. Note that weapons have a maximum applicable damage bonus.
- Skill checks with blacksmithing, intimidate, stoneworking and woodworking

**Ability Checks:** Open stuck door (DC 15), open magically locked door (DC 24), bend bars/lift gates (DC 21)

## Intelligence (Int)

Memory, reasoning and learning ability. Add the standard mod to:

- DCs to resist Mage spells
- Languages known (minimum 1).
- Skill checks with alchemy, ancient languages, appraise, demon & devillore, disguise, forgery, heraldry, herblore, history, leatherworking, magica, poisonlore, runelore and search

**Ability Checks:** Learn spell (DC 12).

## Wisdom (Wis)

Enlightenment, judgement and intuition. Add the standard mod to:

- Willpower saving throws to resist mental attacks
- DCs to resist Cleric and Druid spells
- Skill checks with animal handling, cookery, farming, firecraft, fishing, foraging, herbalism, hunting, navigation, religion, runecarving, sailing, tracking and wound treatment

**Ability Checks:** Prepare cleric spell (DC 4). If this check fails, the spell cannot be used for the day. Note that clerics with Wisdom 13+ succeed automatically.

|  Wis  | Bonus Cleric Spells |
| :---: | :-----------------: |
| 3–13  |          —          |
| 14–15 |      1st level      |
| 16–17 |      2nd level      |
| 18–19 |      3rd level      |
|  20   |      4th level      |

**Note:** Bonus spells are cumulative, so a Cleric with 17 Wisdom receives two bonus spells: one 1st level and one 2nd level.

## Dexterity (Dex)

Hand-eye coordination, agility and balance. Add the standard mod to:

- Reflex saves to dodge traps and physical hazards
- Attack rolls with bow, crossbow, handgonne, sling, straight sword, curved sword, dagger, spear and whip
- Armor Class. Note that armors may limit the maximum applicable bonus.
- Skill checks with bowyery, calligraphy, climbing, dancing, disarm trap, hide, lockpicking, pickpocketing, poison handling, sneak and tailoring

**Ability Checks:** Long jump (DC = distance – 3). High jump (DC = distance x 5. Distance required to jump-and-grab = target height – character's standing reach).
