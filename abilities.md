# Abilities

## Strength (Str)

Brawn and physical might. Add the standard modifier to:

- Attack rolls with axe, bludgeon, hand-to-hand, polearm, and staff
- Damage rolls with all melee and thrown weapons. Note that weapons have a maximum applicable damage bonus.
- Skill checks with climbing, jumping, metalworking, stoneworking, and woodworking

**Ability Checks:** Open stuck door (DC 15), open magically locked door (DC 24), bend bars/lift gates (DC 21)

**Carrying Capacity:** Increases the load characters are able to carry before becoming encumbered.

## Intelligence (Int)

Memory, reasoning and learning ability. Add the standard modifier to:

- Spellcasting level of alteration, enchantment, evocation, and illusion
- Skill checks with alchemy, ancient languages, appraisal, cartography, disguise, gemcraft, heraldry, history, medicine, poetry, poisonlore, scribing, and searching
- Prime requisite of Mage

**Ability Checks:** Learn magic spell (DC 12)

**Spell Level Limit:** The maximum level of magic spell characters can cast is `Int / 2`. E.g. a character with Intelligence 15 can cast up to 7th level spells.

**Bonus Languages:** Characters with high Intelligence begin with additional languages from their race’s bonus list.

|  Int  | Bonus Languages |
| :---: | :-------------: |
|  1–8  |        0        |
| 9–11  |        1        |
| 12–14 |        2        |
| 15–17 |        3        |
| 18–20 |        4        |

## Wisdom (Wis)

Enlightenment, judgement and self-control. Add the standard modifier to:

- Spellcasting level of abjuration, conjuration, divination, and necromancy
- Skill checks with animal handling, brewing, cooking, farming, firemaking, fishing, foraging, herblore, hunting, listening, navigation, necromancy, painting, pottery, religion, runelore, sailing, tracking, and wound treatment
- Will saves

**Ability Checks:** Cast cleric spell (DC 4). Note that clerics with Wisdom 13+ succeed automatically.

**Bonus Cleric Spells:** Clerics with high Wisdom receive bonus spells. These are cumulative. E.g. a Cleric with 17 Wisdom gains one extra 1st-level and one extra 2nd-level spell per day.

|  Wis  | Bonus Cleric Spells |
| :---: | :-----------------: |
| 1–11  |       (none)        |
| 12–14 |      1st level      |
| 15–17 |      2nd level      |
| 18–20 |      3rd level      |

## Dexterity (Dex)

Hand-eye coordination, agility and balance. Add the standard modifier to:

- Attack rolls with bow, crossbow, curved sword, dagger, flail, handgonne, sling, straight sword and spear
- Armor class. Note that worn armor may limit the maximum applicable bonus.
- Skill checks with clothworking, dancing, fletching, hiding, leatherworking, lockpicking, sleight of hand, sneaking, and trap disarming
- Reflex saves

## Constitution (Con)

Fitness, health and hardiness. Add the standard modifier to:

- Hit point rolls
- Skill checks with distance running, riding, skiing and swimming
- Fortitude saves

**Ability Checks:** System shock/resurrection survival (DC 6)

## Charisma (Cha)

Appearance, leadership and personal magnetism. Add the standard modifier to:

- Retainer loyalty score
- Reaction rolls
- Skill checks with Deception, Etiquette, Intimidation, Leadership, Singing, Persuasion, and Trading

**Retainer Limit:** Characters with high Charisma can attract a greater number of retainers.

|  Cha  | Retainer Limit |
| :---: | :------------: |
|  1–2  |       1        |
|  3–5  |       2        |
|  6–8  |       3        |
| 9–11  |       4        |
| 12–14 |       5        |
| 15–17 |       6        |
| 18–20 |       7        |

## Prime Requisite Experience Bonus

Humans with a very high score in their class's prime requisite ability require fewer experience points (XP) to reach the next level. This modifier is fixed and does not change if the ability score later increases or decreases. Note that some classes do not have a prime requisite ability.

| Prime Requisite | XP Required |
| :-------------: | :---------: |
|      3–15       |    100%     |
|       16        |     95%     |
|       17        |     90%     |
|       18        |     85%     |
