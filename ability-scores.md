# Ability Scores

## Strength

Brawn and physical might. Add the standard modifier to:

- Attack rolls with axe, bludgeon, hand-to-hand, polearm and staff
- Damage rolls with all melee and thrown weapons
- Skill checks with blacksmithing, intimidate, stoneworking and woodworking

**Ability Checks:** Open stuck door (DC 15), open magic door (DC 24), bend bars/lift gates (DC 21)

Strength also determines carrying capacity per movement rate (MV):

| Str. Score | Base MV |  9 MV  |  6 MV  |  3 MV  |
| :--------: | :-----: | :----: | :----: | :----: |
|     3      |  20 lb  | 30 lb  | 40 lb  | 80 lb  |
|    4–5     |  30 lb  | 45 lb  | 60 lb  | 120 lb |
|    6–7     |  40 lb  | 60 lb  | 80 lb  | 160 lb |
|    8–13    |  50 lb  | 75 lb  | 100 lb | 200 lb |
|   14–15    |  60 lb  | 90 lb  | 120 lb | 240 lb |
|   16–17    |  70 lb  | 105 lb | 140 lb | 280 lb |
|     18     |  80 lb  | 120 lb | 160 lb | 320 lb |

## Intelligence

Memory, reasoning and learning ability. Add the standard modifier to:

- DCs to resist alteration, enchantment, evocation and illusion spells
- Skill checks with alchemy, ancient languages, appraise, demon & devillore, disguise, forgery, heraldry, herblore, history, leatherworking, magica, poisonlore, runelore and search

**Ability Checks:** Learn spell (DC 12).

Intelligence also determines a character's starting languages and modifies their experience points (XP) required to level up:

| Int. Score | Languages | XP Required |
| :--------: | :-------: | :---------: |
|     3      |     1     |    115%     |
|    4–5     |     1     |    110%     |
|    6–7     |     1     |    105%     |
|    8–13    |     2     |      —      |
|   14–15    |     3     |     95%     |
|   16–17    |     4     |     90%     |
|     18     |     5     |     85%     |

## Wisdom

Enlightenment, judgement and intuition. Add the standard modifier to:

- Saving throws to resist mental attacks
- DCs to resist abjuration, conjuration, divination and necromancy spells
- Skill checks with animal handling, cookery, farming, firecraft, fishing, foraging, herbalism, hunting, navigation, religion, runecarving, sailing, tracking and wound treatment

A high Wisdom score also provides clerics with bonus spells:

| Wis. Score | Bonus Cleric Spells |
| :--------: | :-----------------: |
|    3–13    |          —          |
|   14–15    |         1st         |
|   16–17    |         2nd         |
|     18     |         3rd         |

**Note:** Bonus spells are cumulative, so a cleric with 17 Wisdom receives one bonus 1st level and one bonus 2nd level spell.

## Dexterity (Dex)

Hand-eye coordination, agility and balance. Add the standard modifier to:

- Attack rolls with bow, crossbow, handgonne, sling, straight sword, curved sword, dagger, spear and whip
- Natural armor class
- Initiative rolls
- Skill checks with bowyery, calligraphy, climbing, dancing, disarm trap, hide, lockpicking, pickpocketing, poison handling, sneak and tailoring
- Saving throws to evade physical attacks
